# 小尺寸坐姿与指尖素材记录

使用内置 image_gen 默认工具模式生成。以用户认可的青春人物概念图和既有鲸鱼娘图集为参考，输出原样复制到本目录，保留 RGBA。SVG / WPF 只在运行时按清单裁切、镜像和合成眼部与指尖，不改写原画。

共同规格：1536 × 1024，3 × 2 格，每格 512 × 512。每位人物两张图：seated-v1 为坐姿生活动作；micro-v1 为桌边细微指尖动作。

鲸鱼娘沿用社区原型上善无形「溟月」、女仆版设计 ZipZipPipe 的署名与 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0。详细许可见 [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)。其余三位依据本项目批准的概念图重新设计。

Keep existing youthful charming cartoon identity. Small ahoge entirely INSIDE every cell. Match seated head proportions of the reference. Every image has exactly two correct hands, no extra fingers or arms. This is quiet finger typing, not drumming, punching, waving or high arm movements.
Keep the headband, fin ears and ONE whale tail safely within each cell. The ahoge must fit with padding.
